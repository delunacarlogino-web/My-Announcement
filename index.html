Important Announcement

The Department Of Education (DepEd)

To All Public and Private Schools

Class Suspension

Due to inclement weather brought about by the southwest monsoon, enhanced by tropical cyclone Maliksi (formerly "Domeng"), classes in Metro Manila and nearby areas have been suspended Thursday (July 2).

Here are the cities that announced class cancellations today.

  • Caloocan - All Levels
  • Malabon - All Levels
  • Makati - All Levels
  • Navotas - All Levels
  • Taguig - Pre-School to Elementary
